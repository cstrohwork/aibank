# Capital Signal Intelligence — Daily Market Brief Editorial Standard

This is the authoritative content template for every new CapitalSignal/daily/YYYY-MM-DD/index.html edition. Place the Market Pulse performance chart FIRST, followed by the editorial sections below. Preserve the unified AIBANK / CAPITAL SIGNAL INTELLIGENCE navigation. Do not duplicate Market Pulse with an additional standalone price card section.

## Role and tone
Write as a senior institutional capital markets analyst, corporate advisory strategist, and macro economist for C-suite executives, institutional portfolio managers, and capital markets professionals. Be precise, quantitative, concise, neutral, and source-driven. No filler, hype, retail slang, or investment recommendations.

## Cadence
- Monday–Friday: dated **morning** brief with heading **[Day of Week], [Month Day, Year]** and six sections below. Futures and macro figures must be timestamped pre-market; previous close must reference the prior U.S. trading session. Do not present post-close data as morning data.
- Saturday: **Saturday, [Month Day, Year] | Week in Review**. Five sections: (1) Weekly Market Close & Asset Performance (2) Global Markets Snapshot (weekly/monthly/YTD table) (3) Weekly Macroeconomic Retrospective (4) Corporate Earnings & Sector Recap (5) Capital Markets ECM/DCM Recap.
- Sunday: **Sunday, [Month Day, Year] | The Week Ahead**. Five sections: (1) Weekly Market Close & Asset Performance Baseline (2) Global Markets Snapshot & Positioning (3) The Macro Calendar, day by day (4) Major Corporate Headlines & Earnings to Watch (5) IPOs & Capital Markets Pipeline. Track Fed speaking/blackout, auctions, lockup expirations, roadshows, issuance windows.

## Monday–Friday sections (after Market Pulse)
1. **Opening Market Projections & Stock Futures** — short risk thesis, ES/NQ/YM/RTY futures, 2Y/10Y/30Y Treasury yields, 10Y–2Y spread in bp, VIX, DXY. Timestamp quotes and identify contracts.
2. **Yesterday's Market Close** — official S&P 500, Dow, Nasdaq Composite, Russell 2000 closes with point and percentage changes; sector leadership, rotation, divergences.
3. **Global Markets Snapshot** — clean table: Nikkei, KOSPI, CSI 300, Hang Seng, STOXX 600, DAX, FTSE 100; regional local-session performance and relevant FX, yields, energy drivers.
4. **Economic Calendar & Macro Data Watch** — timed U.S. releases, consensus vs prior, Fed policy probabilities (with source and contract/date), Brent/WTI, spot gold, Bitcoin, natural gas, shipping transit/insurance developments when material and verified.
5. **Major Corporate Headlines** — sourced developments in hyperscaler capex/FCF, AI compute allocations, custom ASICs, GPU/rack systems, HBM3e/HBM4 pricing (SK Hynix/Micron/Samsung), 800G/1.6T optics, power grid/nuclear/utility PPAs/turbines/transformers, material earnings.
6. **IPOs & Capital Markets Pipeline** — verified S-1/registration and IPO roadshow status, tech/infrastructure unit economics, post-IPO secondary trading, investment-grade debt issuance and borrowing spreads, ECM/DCM windows. Never assert a company filed an S-1 merely because it is rumored (including Anthropic).

## Source and quality controls
- Every substantive time-sensitive claim and numerical market observation must have an attributable link, as-of date/time, unit, and relevant comparison basis. Use exchange/issuer/SEC/Federal Reserve/Treasury primary sources where possible; credible reporting for contextual analysis.
- Distinguish live/rolling 24-hour crypto prices from fixed U.S. equity cash closes; no fabricated futures, yields, forecasts, Fed probabilities, sector returns, or issuance volumes.
- Show **Unavailable / Not verified** where a metric is missing; do not replace missing data with an estimate or generic assertion.
- Market Pulse covers equity indices, rates, VIX, Brent, WTI, BTC, ETH and USD index as verified; show positive/negative performance clearly. Its values must align to the edition's reporting period and be labeled if live.
- Keep institutional ownership intelligence in the separate Investor Spotlight publication; do not add an extra daily section unless expressly requested.
- Archive links and dates must point to actual published issues. Maintain mobile and desktop navigation and existing archive.
