1. U.S. Equity Market Close

Stocks declined again Tuesday. The S&P 500 fell 1.03%, the Dow lost 1.64%, and the Nasdaq Composite declined 0.74%, according to the Wall Street Journal's November 17 close report. The Dow experienced the largest percentage loss of the three benchmarks. Source.
