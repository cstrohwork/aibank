1. U.S. Equity Close

U.S. shares finished sharply lower, led by AI-related and semiconductor stocks. The Nasdaq's 2.49% decline exceeded the S&P 500's 1.92% loss and the Dow's 1.41% decline, reflecting technology concentration risk. These are official-session benchmark levels reported by Reuters, not futures indications.

2. Sector and Cross-Asset Performance

Technology and semiconductor names led the decline; a full verified eleven-sector S&P 500 percentage ranking was not available at publication. Nvidia fell about 4.1%, while defensives including food and some healthcare names held up better. Brent fell 3.5% to $92.44; Treasury yields edged lower according to AP, with the 10-year reported around 5.13%. No synchronized dollar, volatility, gold or digital-asset close is asserted.

Sources: AP market recap; Reuters market recap.

4. Corporate and Market-Moving Developments

Nvidia shares extended their decline amid uncertainty surrounding the company's leadership transition and AI infrastructure valuations. Reuters and AP reported continued investor scrutiny of AI spending, potential restrictions affecting advanced chips and cloud infrastructure, and Nvidia's upcoming earnings. Tyson Foods was among the notable gainers after stronger-than-expected results, while Summit Therapeutics declined after a regulatory setback.

Sources: Reuters; AP.
