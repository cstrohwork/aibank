1. U.S. Equity Market Close

The S&P 500 declined 3.70% and entered correction territory, more than 10% below its October high. Nasdaq Composite lost 5.28%, turning negative year to date. Dow fell 1.93% and Russell 2000 approximately 2.76%. The S&P recorded its seventh consecutive down session. AP closing report, November 20.

2. Sector and Cross-Asset Performance

Semiconductors and AI infrastructure led declines. Nvidia fell 13.8%, AMD 13.3%, Micron 13.9% and Broadcom 12.1%, according to AP. Defensive consumer staples and healthcare outperformed. Treasury yields fell amid demand for safer assets, while Brent crude rose on geopolitical concerns. Precise sector index returns, Treasury settlement yields, VIX, DXY, oil settlements and digital-asset closes were not independently reconciled and are withheld. AP, November 20.

3. Material Market-Moving News

Investors continued to reassess AI infrastructure capital spending, prospective returns and stretched valuations. Anthropic fell 9.1% on its second public trading day to $88.30, versus its $83 IPO offering price. Broad weakness persisted despite strong Nvidia results earlier in the week. WSJ, November 20 · AP, November 20.
