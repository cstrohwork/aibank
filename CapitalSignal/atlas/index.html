What these numbers mean

EAUM lens: the value shown is reported, matched-identifier equity exposure from the reconciled SEC cache. It excludes unsupported identifiers, options, non-share 13F positions and invalid values; N-PORT includes supported equity categories. It is not total institution-wide equity AUM. Exclusions are shown for each investor. Different managers can report overlapping discretion, so their values are not added together.

Manager and fund views stay separate. Each screen uses one exact report date; latest-restated disclosures can contain later amendments and do not establish what was known on the reporting date. Investor counts cover selected cached records only. Distinct CUSIPs can include multiple securities of one issuer. Shares are reported positions, not shares-outstanding ownership percentages. No current market price, trading intent, style or active/passive classification is inferred.

Security drilldown requires one unambiguous selected 13F accession, explicit USD units and a match between its holdings and the published summary. Multi-version selections and individual fund positions remain source-linked when that gate cannot be met. All records come from public SEC filings; coverage and normalization remain incomplete.
