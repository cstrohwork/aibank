# Investor intelligence implementation

Run `python scripts/investor_intelligence.py` after fund ownership refresh. Output is `CapitalSignal/data/intelligence/investor_readiness.json`. This runs offline against cached actual fund and manager data and does not alter editorial profiles. The initial snapshot contains 210 distinct latest funds and 4,555 managers. No documentary mandates are available in the existing cache: all 210 fund orientations remain unknown. Fund names, ETF wrappers and benchmark constituent overlap are not promoted to verified passive mandates.

## Documentary mandate inputs

Use `--mandates PATH` with JSON `{"mandates":[...]}`. Each record needs `fund_id`, `source_url`, `evidence_excerpt`, `available_date`, and `effective_from`; optional `effective_to` is exclusive. Optional classification fields are `orientation` (`active` or `passive`), `implementation` (`index`), `strategy` (`public_equity`, `private_equity`, `venture_capital`, `hedge_fund`, `alternative`), and `benchmark_id`. Use dated prospectuses, regulatory disclosures or attributable manager documents. Do not invent effective dates from a retrieval date. Evidence must have been available by the portfolio measurement date. Conflicting simultaneously valid classification evidence is quarantined.

## Classification API

`classify_portfolio(metrics, mandate, as_of)` preserves `editorial_style` separately and returns independent valuation, growth, income, concentration, implementation, strategy axes, factor style, primary style, orientation, reasons, coverage and sources. The taxonomy precedence follows `STYLE_METHODOLOGY.md` while retaining simultaneous attributes.

Metrics require `as_of`, `benchmark_as_of` equal to the measurement date and `source_urls`. Value/growth classification additionally requires `both_dimensions_coverage_pct >= 70`, at least two valid factors in each dimension (`value_factor_count`, `growth_factor_count`), and portfolio weighted `value_score` / `growth_score` on a 0–100 percentile scale. The factor engine must ensure these counts and joint coverage describe eligible securities, not factors scattered across different positions. This API does not manufacture percentile scores.

Optional inputs follow the existing methodology: `sector_coverage_pct`, `largest_sector`, `largest_sector_pct` (strictly above 75%); `valuation_coverage_pct`, `earnings_yield`, `benchmark_earnings_yield`, `fcf_yield`, `benchmark_fcf_yield`, `sector_relative_value_score`; `growth_coverage_pct`, `growth_rate_pct`, `benchmark_growth_rate_pct`; `forward_coverage_pct`, `forward_pe`, `benchmark_forward_pe`, `forward_eps_growth_pct`, `benchmark_forward_eps_growth_pct`; `yield_coverage_pct`, `dividend_yield`, `benchmark_dividend_yield`, and `yield_basis=regular_indicated_forward`. Yield inputs are fractions; growth inputs are percentage points. PEG uses aggregate positive P/E divided by aggregate positive forward growth in percentage points. Missing or nonfinite inputs cannot qualify. Negative market growth does not trigger a ratio-based aggressive-growth classification.

Index alignment requires documentary benchmark evidence plus `benchmark_id`, `index_comparison_coverage_pct`, `index_overlap_pct` and `active_share_pct`; overlap alone never changes orientation. Verified hedge-fund/alternative strategy remains an independent attribute when factors are unresolved; lack of factors alone does not classify a manager Alternative.

`rollup_orientation(funds, institution_equity_assets_usd, as_of)` requires unique fund IDs, verified same-date manager relationships, asset evidence and mandates. Percentages use verified institution equity assets as denominator. It does not renormalize a sampled fund subset to 100%. Requires at least 70% of institution equity assets in one orientation to call active/passive; a sufficiently covered mixed institution remains mixed. Above-denominator sums and duplicate funds are quarantined. Caller must pass a verified institution denominator, never a 13F exposure proxy.

## Targeting

Use `--targets PATH` with `{"issuer":{...},"candidates":[...]}` to evaluate an explicitly requested issuer universe. `score_target(candidate, issuer)` returns a provisional prioritization score and component explanations, not a purchase probability. No issuer requests or candidate targets are invented by the default job.

Candidate fields: `investor_id`, `as_of`, documentary `mandate`, `fundamental_coverage_pct`, `peer_fit_score` / `peer_fit_score_evidence`, `mandate_fit_score` / `mandate_fit_score_evidence`, `verified_equity_assets_usd`, `max_position_pct`, `existing_position_usd` (including a documented zero), `proposed_position_usd`, `source_urls`. Issuer fields: `issuer_id`, `as_of`, `average_daily_dollar_volume_usd`, `max_participation_pct`, `max_days_to_build`, `source_urls`. Candidate and issuer dates must match. All amounts are USD. Fit scores must be attributable to documented upstream peer/mandate comparisons; this module checks their presence and range but cannot establish the truth of caller-supplied evidence.

Coverage below 70%, unavailable mandates, missing evidence, mandate capacity breaches and liquidity breaches block scoring. Available capacity = verified equity assets × explicit position limit minus existing issuer exposure. Days to build = proposed position / (average daily dollar volume × participation limit). Eligible scores weight peer fit 40%, mandate fit 30%, capacity headroom 15%, and liquidity headroom 15%. Weights and thresholds are provisional, exposed in output, and require calibration before representing commercial targeting quality. Existing issuer exposure is a mandatory dated input; this first version does not infer redemptions or willingness to buy.

## Validation and remaining dependencies

`python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_investor_intelligence.py -v` covers coverage boundaries, date mismatch, future/expired evidence, sector threshold, index overlap, PEG sign, special dividends, immutable inputs, firm rollup denominators, invalid values, capacity and liquidity gates. Actual classification and targeting remain blocked until documentary mandates, same-date factors/benchmarks, market liquidity and verified institution mappings are populated. This implementation does not claim those missing datasets are complete.

## Continuing mandate acquisition

Every run also writes `investor_mandate_queue.json`, currently covering 30 real SEC registrants and their latest cached funds. Run `python scripts/investor_intelligence.py --discover-mandates 3 --fetch-prospectuses` to advance three queued registrants. Discovery checkpoints are retained across later default runs; queued registrants take priority over transient failures. Submissions responses identify actual recent 497, 497K, 485APOS, 485BPOS, N-1A and N-2 document URLs, accession numbers and filing availability dates; historical submission file references are retained when older evidence is needed. Up to three documents per registrant are fetched, with bounded strategy/objective excerpts saved for review. Existing successful discovery rows are retained rather than repeatedly downloaded.

Candidate excerpts remain unverified until a specific fund is attributed, the strategy claim is established in context, and an actual effective date is documented. A multi-fund trust prospectus cannot automatically establish every series' mandate. SEC access failures are saved with the affected registrant or document for retry; inaccessible evidence never causes a fund to become active/passive. The optional discovery fetch respects `SEC_USER_AGENT`, uses no account/credential and makes sequential paced requests. Source discovery does not claim exhaustive historical prospectus ingestion.
