How to interpret this workbench

Issuer, target, meeting, watchlist and comparison tools use the existing exact-version validated sample of the 50 largest cached manager candidates at one reporting date. This is incomplete coverage, not all investors. Search uses filing security labels and CUSIPs; ticker matching is not inferred. Securities and share classes remain distinct.

Peer targets are managers present in the validated sample who hold at least one selected peer CUSIP and do not report the selected subject CUSIP in that same sample. This does not establish institution-wide non-ownership, investment intent, available capital, style or active/passive classification. Rank is peer count descending, then summed peer portfolio weights. Peer selection is your research judgment.

Portfolio similarity is the sum of minimum CUSIP weights held by each of two managers, from 0% to 100%. Watchlist flags use a 5% within-manager commitment threshold or at most two sampled holders; they are review prompts, not buy/sell signals. No trade flows, price returns, ownership percentage of shares outstanding or liquidity estimates are inferred.

Filing evidence review uses the newest manager Atlas period, which may differ from the security sample. Missing timestamps identify a limit for point-in-time research. Watchlists and meeting shortlists stay in this browser. A saved watchlist checkpoint can be compared with the next loaded sample; differences reflect cache/sample changes as well as disclosures. This page never runs unattended background monitoring.

All tools read the published files on each page load, so successful Atlas rebuilds feed the workbench without a separate dataset. Exports include reporting dates, checkpoint and sample limits. Security evidence · Period manifest
