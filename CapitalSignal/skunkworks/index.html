Data, definitions & calculation limits

The EAUM lens is reported matched-CUSIP equity exposure in the reconciled SEC cache, not total institution-wide AUM. Manager and fund lenses remain separate. Different managers can report overlapping discretion; no combined institutional AUM or market share is calculated. Security counts are distinct CUSIPs, not issuers.

Each general analysis uses one exact reporting date. Comparisons use identical entity identifiers at two explicitly chosen dates, with a visible matched cohort. Changes can reflect prices, disclosure coverage, corporate actions and holdings changes; they do not establish trading flows, turnover or performance. The cache uses latest-restated records and does not represent point-in-time investable history.

Security analyses use a separate fixed sample: the 50 largest managers in the newest published Atlas manager period are candidates. Cached positions must match the entity, report date, single selected accession, explicit USD units, total value and CUSIP count. Failed candidates are excluded. Zero-exposure portfolios are excluded from weight metrics. Each CUSIP has at most one holder entry per validated manager. Holder counts and weights describe this sample, not the whole institutional market. Security names are filing labels; identical issuers may have multiple CUSIPs. No ownership percentage of shares outstanding is inferred.

Filing delays measure report date to the latest selected filing date, not ingestion speed. A lack of recorded exclusions does not certify source completeness. Every chart has an equivalent table and complete CSV export. Charts show at most 24 categories or ranked rows; cards show at most seven. Empty analyses are labeled explicitly. CSV cells are protected against spreadsheet formula injection.
