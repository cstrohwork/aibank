Weekly assessment

Equities recovered despite a sharp rates reset and semiconductor leadership shock. The S&P 500 gained 2.5% over November 9–13, even as Friday's session fell 0.4%. Rising Treasury yields and uncertainty around NVIDIA's leadership increased the importance of balance-sheet resilience, capital costs and issuer guidance.

Macro and market drivers

The 10-year Treasury yield finished around 5.15%, roughly 30 basis points above November 6; the two-year yield also rose sharply. October CPI increased 0.4% month over month, increasing expectations of a more restrictive Federal Reserve path. These yield moves are percentage-point changes, not bond investment returns. FT, Nov. 13; weekly closes.

Technology and semiconductors diverged: NVIDIA lost approximately 12.8% over the week following Jensen Huang's departure, while the semiconductor index gained approximately 8.1% over the same period. AMD and Broadcom were among weekly gainers but sold off sharply Friday. Weekly constituent review; Reuters, Nov. 13.

Corporate transactions and capital formation

IPO: Windward Bio raised approximately $175 million, pricing roughly 10.3 million shares at $17. Reuters, Nov. 13.

M&A: BCP Investment Corporation agreed to acquire Investcorp Credit Management BDC; reported terms value Investcorp at approximately 90% of adjusted NAV. The proposed transaction illustrates continued BDC consolidation. Reuters, Nov. 13.

Capital markets services: Morgan Stanley agreed to acquire transfer agent Vinyl Equity, with financial terms undisclosed and a targeted first-quarter 2027 close. Barron's, Nov. 10.

Buy-side and issuer relations

Investor-relations teams should prepare for renewed questions on financing assumptions, rate sensitivity and AI-related capital spending. Next week's watchpoints

October retail sales, industrial production and housing starts will test the growth picture after the inflation surprise.
