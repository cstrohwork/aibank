Data and compliance boundaries

Intended inputs include SEC EDGAR 13F-HR, SC 13D/G, issuer filings, and appropriately licensed public market data. Each output should retain accession number, filing date, reporting period, issuer identifier and transformation history. No proprietary holdings, personal data, material nonpublic information, trade instructions or investment recommendations.

Important: 13F disclosures are generally quarterly and delayed; they do not represent a complete shareholder register. The current analyzer does not fetch, reconcile or verify SEC filings, and does not estimate liquidity or activist probability.
