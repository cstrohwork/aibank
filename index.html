Automating Deal Analysis from opportunity identification through shareholder engagement

AIBank provides detailed, data-driven analysis for every part of a transaction, from financial modeling and deal price analysis through shareholder risk identification, predictive shareholder vote analysis, activist risks analysis, and shareholder targeting outreach. Built for M&A advisors, M&A teams, and IR teams who need to know shareholder deal support probability before they pitch it.

What it does

01

Deal financial modeling

Dynamic M&A modeling grounded in financial filings, but customizable by users. See synergy modifications impact deal impact and results in real time.

02

Shareholder risk identification

Identify which institutional investors are positioned to support or oppose a deal, prior to the vote.

03

Key risk factor analysis

Surface the governance and voting patterns that predict friction.

04

Investor base modeling

A full picture of shareholder ownership, who owns, who is at risk of selling, and why.

05

Investor outreach strategy

Identify which at-risk shareholders can be influenced, while prioritizing non-holders for executive outreach and engagement.
