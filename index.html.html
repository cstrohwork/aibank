What it does

01

Deal financial modeling

Standard M&A modeling grounded in the target's actual filed fundamentals, not a manually rebuilt spreadsheet.

02

Shareholder risk identification

See which institutional holders are positioned to support, block, or swing a deal before you're in the room.

03

Key risk factor analysis

Surface the governance and voting patterns that predict friction, ahead of due diligence.

04

Investor base modeling

A full picture of who holds a company today, built from ownership and voting records, not a purchased contact list.

05

Investor outreach strategy

Prioritize which holders to approach, based on how they've actually voted on comparable situations.
